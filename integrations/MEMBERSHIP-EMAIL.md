# Membership team automatic reply — draft

**Subject:** Thank you for contacting the ZAPD Membership Team

Dear colleague,

Thank you for contacting the Zimbabwe Association of Paediatric Dentistry (ZAPD). We have received your email, and our membership team will review your enquiry and respond as soon as possible.

If you are interested in joining ZAPD, please complete the membership application at https://zapd.org.zw/join. If you have already applied, please include your full name and the email address used for your application so that we can assist you.

Our team can help with membership categories, eligibility, fees and the next steps in the application process. This automatic acknowledgement does not confirm membership approval.

Thank you for your interest in supporting children's oral health in Zimbabwe.

Kind regards,
ZAPD Membership Team
Zimbabwe Association of Paediatric Dentistry
info@zapd.org.zw

---

This is a draft, not an active email automation. Before enabling it, verify the application URL on your live website. Configure it in the email provider that hosts the membership team's mailbox; the site's `mailto:` link cannot send automatic replies. If `info@zapd.org.zw` also handles other enquiries, scope the rule to membership messages or use a dedicated membership mailbox. The mailbox provider determines the exact setup steps.

# ZAPD image source

Requested source: https://www.figma.com/make/U4xGqAkKRvP3H2xKe2RGut/ZAPD

The public Figma Make preview was inspected on 12 October 2026. Its original image set is available in this export under `src/assets`. The site combines these supplied images with complementary stock photographs of smiling children and pastel dental still-life images from the supplied collection. See `stock/CREDITS.md` for sources. The painted-hands child is used once, on the homepage.

Confirmed preview assets include:
- `child-brushing.jpeg`: child brushing her teeth
- `happy-child.jpeg`: smiling child with colourful painted hands
- `oral-health-education.jpeg`: toothbrushing demonstration with a teaching model
- `child-dentist-parent.jpg`: child and parent at a dental visit
- `dental-blue.jpg`: playful tooth models on a blue background
- Original ZAPD logos and member portraits

The filename pairs in this export do not always refer to the same image; `happyChild.jpg` and `happy-child.jpeg`, for example, are different photos. Use the reviewed hyphenated originals when updating these sections. Stock appearances are illustrative and do not establish anyone's nationality or association membership.

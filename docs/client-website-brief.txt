The  homepage  should  introduce  four  key  areas:  
Prevention Promoting  good  oral-health  practices  and  early  preventive  care  to  give  every  child  the  best  possible  start.  
Education Providing  parents,  caregivers  and  dental  professionals  with  accessible,  evidence-based  information  about  
children's
 
oral
 
health.
 
Professional  Development Creating  opportunities  for  dental  professionals  to  develop  their  knowledge  and  skills  in  the  care  of  
children.
 
Collaboration Building  a  community  of  dental  professionals  who  can  learn  from  one  another  and  work  together  to  
improve
 
children's
 
oral
 
health
 
in
 
Zimbabwe.
 
Why  Children's  Oral  Health  Matters  
•
 A  child's  oral  health  is  an  important  part  of  their  overall  health,  development  and  quality  of  life.  
•
 Good  oral-health  habits  begin  early.  Regular  dental  care,  prevention  and  timely  management  of  
dental
 
problems
 
can
 
help
 
children
 
grow
 
with
 
healthy
 
smiles
 
and
 
establish
 
positive
 
attitudes
 
towards
 
oral
 
health
 
that
 
last
 
into
 
adulthood.
 
•
 ZAPD  seeks  to  support  children,  families  and  dental  professionals  through  education,  collaboration  
and
 
the
 
promotion
 
of
 
quality
 
paediatric
 
dental
 
care.

3.  ABOUT  ZAPD  
Who  We  Are  
The  Zimbabwe  Association  of  Paediatric  Dentistry  (ZAPD)  is  a  professional  association  dedicated  to  
improving
 
the
 
oral
 
health
 
of
 
children
 
in
 
Zimbabwe.
 
The  Association  provides  a  platform  for  dental  professionals  with  an  interest  in  paediatric  dentistry  to  
connect,
 
share
 
knowledge,
 
support
 
professional
 
development
 
and
 
contribute
 
to
 
improved
 
oral-health
 
outcomes
 
for
 
children.
 
We  recognise  that  caring  for  children  requires  an  approach  that  considers  not  only  their  teeth,  but  also  their  
growth,
 
development,
 
emotional
 
wellbeing,
 
family
 
environment
 
and
 
individual
 
needs.
 
Vision  
A  Zimbabwe  where  every  child  has  the  opportunity  to  grow  with  good  oral  health  and  a  positive  
relationship
 
with
 
dental
 
care.
 
Mission  
To  promote  excellence  in  the  prevention,  education  and  provision  of  paediatric  dental  care  through  
professional
 
development,
 
collaboration
 
and
 
public
 
education.
 
Objectives  
ZAPD  aims  to:  
•
 Promote  the  importance  of  oral  health  from  infancy  through  adolescence.  
•
 Encourage  prevention  and  early  intervention  in  children's  oral  healthcare.  
•
 Promote  evidence-based  approaches  to  paediatric  dental  care.  
•
 Provide  opportunities  for  continuing  professional  development.  
•
 Facilitate  the  sharing  of  knowledge  and  clinical  experience  among  dental  professionals.  
•
 Support  education  of  parents,  caregivers  and  communities  about  children's  oral  health.  
•
 Encourage  collaboration  between  dental  professionals  and  other  healthcare  professionals  involved  in  
the
 
care
 
of
 
children.
 
•
 Support  initiatives  that  contribute  to  improved  oral-health  outcomes  for  children  in  Zimbabwe.  
•
 Create  a  professional  network  for  individuals  with  an  interest  in  paediatric  dentistry.  
Our  Values  
Child-centred  care  |  Prevention  |  Excellence  |  Collaboration  |  Integrity  |  Inclusivity 
There  should  also  be  a  section  for:  
Executive  Committee  
This  will  include  photographs,  names,  professional  titles  and  positions  of  the  ZAPD  Executive  Committee.

4.  PARENTS  &  CAREGIVERS  
This  section  should  be  particularly  user-friendly  and  written  in  accessible  language.  
Your  Child's  Smile  Starts  Early  
Children's  dental  care  begins  long  before  the  first  permanent  teeth  appear.  
Healthy  baby  teeth  are  important  for  eating,  speaking,  development  and  maintaining  space  for  permanent  
teeth.
 
Establishing
 
good
 
oral-health
 
habits
 
early
 
can
 
also
 
help
 
children
 
develop
 
a
 
positive
 
attitude
 
towards
 
dental
 
care.
 
Suggested  Topics  
•
 When  should  my  child  first  see  a  dentist?  
•
 Baby  teeth  matter  
•
 Toothbrushing  
•
 Fluoride  and  toothpaste  
•
 Diet  and  children's  teeth  
•
 Preventing  tooth  decay  
•
 Dental  anxiety  
•
 Dental  emergencies  
•
 Children's  dental  development  
•
 Dental  injuries  
•
 Special  care  needs  
•
 Frequently  asked  questions  
These  can  initially  be  presented  as  article  cards  and  expanded  as  content  is  developed.

6.  MEMBERSHIP  
Become  Part  of  ZAPD  
Membership  of  the  Zimbabwe  Association  of  Paediatric  Dentistry  provides  an  opportunity  to  connect  with  
colleagues
 
who
 
share
 
an
 
interest
 
in
 
children's
 
oral
 
health.
 
Whether  you  are  involved  extensively  in  paediatric  dentistry  or  simply  have  a  strong  interest  in  improving  
the
 
dental
 
care
 
of
 
children,
 
ZAPD
 
provides
 
a
 
platform
 
for
 
learning,
 
collaboration
 
and
 
professional
 
engagement.
 
Proposed  Membership  Categories  
1.  Paediatric  Dentist  Member For  dentists  with  recognised  postgraduate  training/qualification  in  paediatric  dentistry.  
2.  Dentist  Member For  registered  dentists  with  an  interest  in  paediatric  dentistry  and  children's  oral  health.  
3.  Trainee/Registrar  Member For  dentists  undertaking  recognised  postgraduate  training  in  paediatric  dentistry.  
4.  Student  Member For  undergraduate  dental  students.  
5.  Allied  Oral  Health  Professional  Member For  appropriately  registered  dental  therapists,  oral  health  therapists,  oral  hygienists  and  other  relevant  
oral-health
 
professionals.
 
6.  Retired/Emeritus  Member For  retired  members  or  suitably  recognised  senior  members  of  the  profession.  
7.  International/Affiliate  Member For  professionals  outside  Zimbabwe  who  have  an  interest  in  paediatric  dentistry  or  a  professional  
relationship
 
with
 
ZAPD.
 
8.  Honorary  Member For  individuals  recognised  by  ZAPD  for  an  outstanding  contribution  to  children's  oral  health  or  to  the  
Association.
 
The  final  eligibility  requirements,  voting  rights  and  membership  fees  will  be  confirmed  by  the  Association  
and
 
incorporated
 
into
 
the
 
membership
 
application
 
system.

12.  DESIGN  DIRECTION  
The  website  should  feel:  
•
 Professional  
•
 Modern  
•
 Warm  
•
 Child-friendly  without  looking  childish  
•
 Trustworthy  
•
 Clean  and  uncluttered  
•
 Appropriate  for  a  national  professional  dental  association  
We  would  like  to  avoid  an  overly  cartoon-like  children's  website.  The  target  audience  includes  both  
healthcare
 
professionals
 
and
 
parents,
 
so
 
the
 
design
 
should
 
balance
 
warmth
 
with
 
professionalism.
 
Photography  should  preferably  feature  real  children,  families  and  dental  professionals,  where  appropriate  
and
 
with
 
the
 
necessary
 
permissions.
 
The  colour  palette  should  complement  the  ZAPD  logo/branding  once  finalised.  
The  website  should  be:  
•
 Fully  responsive  on  mobile,  tablet  and  desktop  
•
 Easy  to  navigate  
•
 Easy  for  the  Association  to  update  
•
 Search-engine  friendly  
•
 Secure  
•
 Fast-loading  
•
 Accessible  
•
 Capable  of  adding  new  events,  articles  and  resources  over  time

14.  CONTENT  WE  ARE  DELIBERATELY  NOT  INCLUDING  
The  ZAPD  website  should  not  position  the  Association  around:  
•
 Advocacy  for  paediatric  dentistry  to  become  a  separate  recognised  speciality  
•
 Medical  aid/health  insurance  inclusion  advocacy  
•
 Industry  advocacy  
These  are  not  part  of  the  current  positioning  of  the  Association.  
The  focus  should  instead  remain  on:  
Children's  Oral  Health  |  Prevention  |  Education  |  Professional  Development  |  Collaboration  |  
Quality
 
Paediatric
 
Dental
 
Care
